# Design review checklist

- Problem, goals, non-goals and acceptance criteria are linked.
- Actors, trust boundaries, data classification and ownership are explicit.
- At least two options and trade-offs are recorded in an ADR.
- Failure modes, retries, idempotency, timeouts and rollback are modeled.
- Databricks objects and environments have owners, permissions and deployment paths.
- MLflow trace, dataset, scorer, feedback and monitoring interfaces are named.
- Threats, mitigations and residual risks are linked to tests or controls.
- Test strategy includes unit, contract, integration, E2E, performance and adversarial cases as applicable.
- Operational SLOs, cost budgets, alerts and runbook entry points are defined.
